Grid Inventory two inventories

Drag an item to move it, including into the other inventory (it is re-homed there with the same rules as a move: auto-rotate, displace, slide). R while dragging to rotate, right-click to rotate in place, double-click to remove. Combine mode (or Shift) merges the dragged item into the one it is dropped on, in the same or the other inventory.

Add item

Clicking an inventory selects it as the target.

Combine

Drop an item onto another one, in this or the other inventory, to replace both with one new item placed at the target's spot (in the target's inventory). Or hold Shift while dragging.
